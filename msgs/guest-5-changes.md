# 客人消息 · 改期、取消、冲突、损坏

> 消息正文保持英文（发给客人/清洁工/供应商）；说明为中文。

使用方法：复制模板，填好每个 [方括号]，再个性化两句话。事实信息一律来自房源卡。绝不手打门锁密码或各类密码。

## F. 改期、取消、冲突与索赔

### F1. 改期 — 可以

```
Hi [First name], good news — [new dates] are available. I've sent a change request through [channel]; the new total is $[amount] ([difference]). Once you accept, you'll get an updated confirmation.

[Sign-off]
```

### F2. 超出退款期限的取消（无特殊情况）

```
Hi [First name],

I'm sorry your plans have changed. Your booking falls under the [policy name] cancellation policy you agreed to at booking, which means [what the policy provides for these dates].

What we can do: [option that costs little — "move your stay to [lower-demand dates] at no change fee" / "if we rebook your nights, we'll refund the rebooked portion"]. Would either of those help?

[Sign-off]
```

### F3. 特殊情况（不可抗力等）
*适用时遵循该渠道自己的重大突发事件规则*

```
Hi [First name],

I'm so sorry to hear about [circumstance]. Please don't worry about the booking for now.

[Channel] has a process for situations like this — you can submit it here: [channel help page]. [If goodwill is approved: "Separately, we'd like to offer [refund / credit] of $[x] for these dates."]

Take care of yourself, and let us know if there's anything we can do.

[Sign-off]
```

### F4. 日历冲突 — 换房安置（房东方原因）

```
Hi [First name],

I'm very sorry — we've found a booking error on our side affecting your dates at [property nickname], and we can't host you there.

We'd like to move you to [alternative property], [what makes it equal or better: "same size, 0.4 miles away, with a private patio"], at no extra cost. [Plus goodwill: "We'll also refund $[x] for the inconvenience."] Photos and details: [listing link after booking].

If you'd prefer not to move, we'll cancel with a full refund immediately. Please let me know which you'd like — and again, I'm sorry.

[Sign-off]
```

### F5. 损坏或额外清洁 — 首次联系（退房后 24 小时内）
*只讲事实 · 附上证据*

```
Hi [First name],

Thank you for staying with us. During the turnover after your stay, our team found [specific damage / condition], which was in good order at the inspection before your arrival ([date]).

Photos are attached. The [repair / replacement / extra cleaning] costs $[amount] based on [invoice / quote].

We'd appreciate it if you could send this through [channel]'s payment request, which I'll create now. If you think we've got something wrong, please reply — we're happy to talk it through.

[Sign-off]
```
