# 评价回复

> 消息正文保持英文（发给客人/清洁工/供应商）；说明为中文。

几天内回复。点名致谢，内容具体，只讲事实。绝不争辩、不提退款、不透露隐私细节。

## G. 公开评价回复

每条评价都在 3 天内回复。点名致谢，内容具体，只讲事实。绝不争辩、不提退款、不透露隐私细节，也不泄露客人的个人信息。

### G1. 五星评价
```
Thank you, [First name]! We're so glad [specific detail they mentioned — "the garden patio became your morning coffee spot"]. It was a pleasure having you, and we'd love to welcome you back to [property nickname].
```

### G2. 好评中带一点小建议
```
Thank you for the lovely review, [First name], and for the helpful note about [point]. We've already [fact: "added a second set of blackout curtains to the front bedroom"]. We're glad you enjoyed [specific positive], and we hope to host you again.
```

### G3. 中评或差评 — 清洁问题
```
Thank you for your feedback, [First name]. You're right that [specific point] didn't meet our standard, and we're sorry. Since your stay we've [fact: "added a second inspection to every turnover at this home"]. We hope future guests — and you, if you return — see the difference.
```

### G4. 中评或差评 — 设施故障
```
Thank you for staying with us, [First name], and for your patience when [amenity] stopped working. Our technician [fact: "replaced the part the next afternoon"], and we've added [preventive step] to our maintenance schedule. We appreciate the kind words about [positive they mentioned].
```

### G5. 与事实不符的差评（只讲事实，绝不针对个人）

```
Thank you for your review. For future guests: [neutral correction of fact — "the listing notes street parking only, and our check-in message includes the parking map"]. We're always working to make arrival easy, and we're sorry the stay didn't feel right.
```

### G6. 回复私下反馈
```
Hi [First name], thank you for telling us about [point] — this kind of feedback really helps. We've [action taken] so the next guest has a better experience. We appreciate you.

[Sign-off]
```
