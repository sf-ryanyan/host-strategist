# 客人消息 · 退房及之后

> 消息正文保持英文（发给客人/清洁工/供应商）；说明为中文。

使用方法：复制模板，填好每个 [方括号]，再个性化两句话。事实信息一律来自房源卡。绝不手打门锁密码或各类密码。

## E. 离店与入住结束后

### E1. 退房指引（退房前一晚 18:00）

```
Hi [First name], we hope you've had a wonderful stay!

Checkout is tomorrow by [checkout time] [time zone]. Before you go:
• Load and start the dishwasher
• Put trash and recycling in the bins by [location]
• Leave used towels in the bathtub
• Close the windows and lock the door ([how to lock])

No need to strip the beds — we'll take care of the rest. Safe travels!

[Sign-off]
```

### E2. 感谢与邀请评价（退房后 2–4 小时）
*如果客人有未解决的问题，不要发这条，改用 E4*

```
Hi [First name],

Thank you for staying at [property nickname] — it was a pleasure hosting you. [One specific, true detail: "We hope the birthday dinner on the patio was everything you wanted."]

If you have a moment, a review on [channel] helps other travelers and means a lot to our small team.

We'd love to welcome you back.

[Sign-off]
```

### E3. 温和提醒评价（退房后 +3 天，只发一次，且仅限入住顺利时）

```
Hi [First name], we hope the journey home went smoothly. If you haven't had a chance yet, we'd be grateful for a quick review on [channel] whenever it suits you. Thank you again for being such great guests!

[Sign-off]
```

### E4. 入住期间出过问题之后（代替 E2）

```
Hi [First name],

Thank you for staying with us, and thank you again for your patience with the [issue]. We've since [what was done to prevent it: "replaced the heater and added it to our monthly checks"].

If there's anything else you'd like to share with us directly, I'd really welcome it — reply any time.

[Sign-off]
```

### E5. 欢迎再来（退房后 +30 天 — 仅在客人同意且渠道规则允许时）

```
Hi [First name],

We hope you've been well since your stay at [property nickname]. If you're planning another trip to [city], returning guests can book directly at [website] and receive [benefit from settings: "no booking fee" / "flexible check-in"].

We'd be happy to see you again.

[Sign-off]
```
