# 客人消息 · 预订、行程规划、抵达

> 消息正文保持英文（发给客人/清洁工/供应商）；说明为中文。

使用方法：复制模板，填好每个 [方括号]，再个性化两句话。事实信息一律来自房源卡。绝不手打门锁密码或各类密码。

## B. 预订确认与行程规划

### B1. 欢迎与确认（预订后 1 小时内）

```
Hi [First name],

Thank you for booking [property nickname] — we're so glad you're coming!

Your stay: [check-in day, date] from [check-in time] to [checkout day, date] at [checkout time] ([time zone]) · [n] guests.

Here's what happens next:
• A week before you arrive, we'll check in about your plans and any special requests.
• The day before arrival, you'll receive the address, parking details and your personal entry code.

If you have any questions in the meantime, just reply here — we usually answer within [response time].

[Sign-off]
```

### B2. 行程规划（入住前 7 天；如预订时已不足 7 天，则预订时发送）

```
Hi [First name],

Your trip to [city] is one week away! A few quick questions so everything is ready for you:

• Roughly what time do you expect to arrive?
• Are you celebrating anything, or traveling for work?
• Is there anything that would make your stay easier — a travel crib, high chair, extra towels, accessibility needs?

If helpful, we can also offer [only add-ons in settings: early check-in from [time] for $[x] / grocery pre-stock / mid-stay clean]. Just let me know.

[Sign-off]
```

## C. 抵达前与抵达

### C1. 入住信息（入住前 1 天，12:00 前）

```
Hi [First name],

Tomorrow's the day! Here's everything you need for an easy arrival.

ADDRESS
[Street address], [City], [State] [ZIP]

PARKING
[Exact parking instructions — where, how many cars, what to avoid]

GETTING IN (from [check-in time] [time zone])
1. [Step 1 — find the door]
2. Enter your personal code: [DOOR CODE — sent via lock app]
3. [Step 3 — how to lock when leaving]

WI-FI
Network: [network name] · Password: [in the guest guide / sent here, per company setting]

YOUR GUIDE
Everything about the home, the neighborhood and checkout is here: [guidebook link]

A few house rules that matter most: [max guests] guests maximum, quiet hours [quiet hours], no smoking or parties.

Safe travels — message us any time if you need anything.

[Sign-off]
```

### C2. 欢迎入住（入住时间）

```
Hi [First name], welcome to [property nickname]!

We hope you find everything ready. If anything isn't right, or you can't find something, message us here — we're on call [hours or "24/7"] and usually reply within [response time].

Enjoy your evening.

[Sign-off]
```

### C3. 入住次日问候（抵达次日 09:30）

```
Good morning, [First name] — just checking in. Is everything in the home as you expected?

If anything needs attention, tell us now and we'll sort it today.

[Sign-off]
```

### C4. 提前入住 — 可以 / 不行
*代码块内的 CONFIRMED（可以）和 NOT POSSIBLE（不行）是版本标签，只发送其中一个版本，标签本身不发。*

```
CONFIRMED
Hi [First name], good news — the home will be ready early, so you're welcome to check in from [time] [time zone]. [If paid: "The early check-in fee of $[x] has been sent as a request on [channel]."] Your code will work from that time.

NOT POSSIBLE
Hi [First name], I'm sorry — we have guests leaving that morning and our team needs the full time to prepare the home, so check-in stays at [check-in time]. [Offer what is true: "You're welcome to drop your bags on the porch from [time]" / "[café name] around the corner is a lovely place to wait."] I'll message you the moment it's ready if we finish early.

[Sign-off]
```
