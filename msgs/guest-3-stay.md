# 客人消息 · 入住期间

> 消息正文保持英文（发给客人/清洁工/供应商）；说明为中文。

使用方法：复制模板，填好每个 [方括号]，再个性化两句话。事实信息一律来自房源卡。绝不手打门锁密码或各类密码。

## D. 入住期间

### D1. 入住中途问候（住 5 晚及以上，第 3 天）

```
Hi [First name], hope the stay is going well!

A quick note: fresh towels and linens are in [location]. If you'd like a mid-stay clean, we can arrange one on [day] for $[x] — just reply "yes".

[One genuine local tip, verified current.]

[Sign-off]
```

### D2. 问题 — 确认收到（10 分钟内）

```
Hi [First name], thank you for letting us know about the [issue] — I'm sorry, that's not the stay we want for you.

We're on it now. [One simple step if useful: "Could you send a quick photo of the [panel / thermostat display]?" / "Could you try switching the [breaker labeled X] off and on?"]

I'll update you by [time, time zone] at the latest.

[Sign-off]
```

### D3. 问题 — 进展更新（务必在承诺时间前发出，即使没有新消息）

```
Hi [First name], an update on the [issue]: [what has happened — e.g. "our technician is booked for 4:00–5:00 pm today"].

[What the guest needs to do, if anything: "They'll come to the side gate; you don't need to be home."]

Next update by [time]. Thank you for your patience.

[Sign-off]
```

### D4. 问题 — 已解决与补偿

```
Hi [First name], the [issue] is fixed — [confirmation, e.g. "the hot tub is back at 102°F"]. Could you let me know it's working for you too?

We're sorry for the trouble. [Proportional gesture from module 01 §6.2: "As a thank-you for your patience, we've sent a refund of $[x] for the evening without the hot tub" / "Late checkout until 1:00 pm is yours if you'd like it."]

[Sign-off]
```

### D5. 噪音 — 礼貌提醒（首次警报，10 分钟内）

```
Hi [First name], just a friendly note — the home's noise monitor is showing raised levels, and the neighbors are close by. Could you keep things a little quieter, especially after [quiet-hours start]?

Thank you — enjoy the rest of your evening.

[Sign-off]
```

### D6. 噪音 — 第二次警报（语气坚定，先尝试打电话之后再发）

```
Hi [First name], we've tried to call you — the noise monitor is still showing high levels after our earlier message.

As agreed in the house rules when you booked, quiet hours are [quiet hours] and the maximum is [max guests] guests. Please bring the volume down now and make sure only registered guests are in the home.

If the issue continues, our on-call manager will need to visit the property, following [channel]'s rules.

[Sign-off]
```

### D7. 续住邀请（客人住期前后有相邻空档）

```
Hi [First name], if you'd like a little longer in [city], the home is free on [night(s)] after your stay. We can offer [n] extra night(s) for $[total incl. fees] — just say the word and I'll send the change request through [channel].

[Sign-off]
```

### D8. 延迟退房 — 回复
*代码块内的 YES（可以）和 NO（不行）是版本标签，只发送其中一个版本，标签本身不发。*

```
YES
Hi [First name], happy to — your checkout is now [time] [time zone]. [If paid: "The $[x] late-checkout fee will come as a request on [channel]."] Enjoy the slower morning!

NO
Hi [First name], I'm sorry — we have guests arriving the same day and need the full time to prepare. Checkout stays at [checkout time]. [If true: "You're welcome to leave bags in [location] until [time]."]

[Sign-off]
```
