# 客人消息 · 询问与预订前

> 消息正文保持英文（发给客人/清洁工/供应商）；说明为中文。

使用方法：复制模板，填好每个 [方括号]，再个性化两句话。事实信息一律来自房源卡。绝不手打门锁密码或各类密码。

## A. 询问与预订前

### A1. 合格询问 — 回答并邀请预订（绿灯）
*渠道：平台内 · 时间：在询问回复服务标准内*

```
Hi [First name],

Thanks for reaching out — yes, [direct answer to their first question].

[Answer to second question, if any.] For a [purpose of trip, e.g. "work week" / "family weekend"], guests especially love [one relevant highlight: "the quiet desk by the window and 500 Mbps fibre" / "the fenced garden"].

A couple of things to know: the home sleeps up to [max guests], quiet hours are [quiet hours], and [one rule that matters for them, e.g. "pets aren't allowed"].

I've pre-approved your dates ([check-in] – [checkout]) for the next 24 hours, so you can book whenever you're ready.

[Sign-off]
```

### A2. 批准前先确认（黄灯）

```
Hi [First name],

Thanks for your message — [date range] is available and we'd be glad to host you.

So we can make sure the home is right for your stay, could you tell me:
• How many adults and children will be staying?
• What brings you to [city] — work, family, a celebration?
• [Specific question, e.g. "Will you be bringing a pet? We can accept one dog under 40 lb with approval."]

Once I hear back I'll send you a pre-approval straight away.

[Sign-off]
```

### A3. 庆祝活动或可能办聚会 — 提前讲清规则（黄灯，有聚会风险信号）
*仅在多个信号同时出现时使用（见模块 01 §2.2）。*

```
Hi [First name],

Congratulations on the [occasion] — that sounds like a lovely reason for a trip.

Just so everyone has a great stay: [property nickname] is a home on a quiet residential street, so we don't allow parties or events, the maximum is [max guests] guests including day visitors, and quiet hours are [quiet hours]. The home has a disclosed noise-level monitor (it measures volume only — nothing is recorded).

If a relaxed stay for [number] guests fits your plans, I'm happy to pre-approve. Could you confirm the number of guests?

[Sign-off]
```

### A4. 婉拒 — 超员或办活动（红灯）

```
Hi [First name],

Thank you for thinking of us. The home is set up for a maximum of [max guests] guests, and we're not able to host events, so I'm sorry to say it isn't the right fit for this trip.

[Optional, if true: "We also manage [property nickname], which sleeps [n] — happy to check those dates for you."]

I hope you find a great place for your celebration.

[Sign-off]
```

### A5. 客人要求平台外付款或沟通

```
Hi [First name],

Thanks for asking. For your protection and ours, all bookings, payments and messages for this home stay on [channel] — that way you have the platform's full support and payment protection.

I'm happy to answer anything here, and you can book directly on the listing whenever you're ready.

[Sign-off]
```

### A6. 日期不可订 — 提供替代方案

```
Hi [First name],

Thank you for your interest — I'm sorry, [dates] are already booked.

The closest open dates are [alternative dates]. [If applicable: "We also have [property nickname], [short description, distance], open for your exact dates."]

Would either of these work for you?

[Sign-off]
```

### A7. 不允许宠物时收到宠物请求

```
Hi [First name],

Thanks for asking about [pet name / your dog]. I'm sorry — this home isn't able to accept pets, [optional factual reason: "as part of the building rules"].

[Only if true: "Our [property nickname] is pet-friendly and available for your dates if that helps."] Assistance animals are always welcome — just let us know so we can prepare.

[Sign-off]
```
